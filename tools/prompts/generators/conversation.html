Conversation's scenario skeleton. Determine a topic open to a dialogue between 2 and 5 people and characters appropriate to that topic and situation. Assign a role to the user in the instructions that is appropriate to the topic and situation. Then, the user will speak in accordance with this role and we will take this into consideration when making our evaluation. Instead of simple scenario like how is day going, create a situation specific to the user (if we have information, it can be from their relevant fields). Maybe a philosophical discussion, maybe a dialogue between drivers after a car accident, maybe a doctor-patient interview. Create a situation with creative examples and place the user there nicely.
